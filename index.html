The Challenge

Where Luxury
Gets Lost Online

Breuninger has defined premium fashion retail in the DACH region for over 140 years. Their in-store advisors — deeply knowledgeable, warm, unhurried — are the brand's greatest competitive asset. The digital channel failed to replicate even a fraction of that experience.

Discovery was generic. Product pages were transactional. Customers browsed alone, without guidance, context, or curatorial voice. The result: lower conversion, higher returns, and a growing gap between the brand's physical prestige and its digital reality.

"We knew the product. We knew the customer. What we couldn't figure out was how to bring our people's expertise into a screen."

— Breuninger Digital Strategy Lead

Key Pain Points Identified

  • No personalised entry point — every user saw an identical homepage regardless of their style profile or history
  • Discovery relied on keyword search — customers who didn't know exactly what they wanted had nowhere to start
  • Size and fit information was static and text-heavy, leading to speculative purchases and frequent returns
  • No sense of occasion-based dressing — the platform sold items, not outfits
  • Mobile experience felt like a shrunken desktop — not designed for the luxury shopper's real context
  • No feedback loop — purchases and returns generated zero signal for future recommendations

Design Process

From Insight
to Prototype

01
Research

Discover & Define

Contextual interviews with 18 Breuninger customers, shadowing 6 in-store stylist sessions, and a competitive audit of Mytheresa, Farfetch, and Net-a-Porter's AI features.

02
Figma

Ideate & Prototype

Rapid wireframing of 7 concept directions. Converged on an AI Concierge model — a conversational stylist that surfaces throughout the journey, not just at search.

03
Nano Banana · VEO3

Simulate & Test

Used Nano Banana for AI-driven prototype interactions; VEO3 to generate photorealistic product context video for the styling flows, eliminating the need for live model shoots.

04
Adobe Premiere Pro

Narrative & Handoff

Assembled the stakeholder demo reel in Premiere Pro — weaving the prototype walkthrough with AI-generated context footage and user testing highlights.

Reflection

What I Would
Do Differently

Every project teaches. Three things I'd approach differently with hindsight — and carry forward as new principles.

01

Test Aversion Earlier

I underestimated how uncomfortable some luxury shoppers are with AI-surfaced personalization. Earlier research on trust and data sensitivity would have shaped the concierge's consent UX from day one, not week nine.

02

Design the Empty State

Cold-start is the hardest state in any personalised experience. I spent too long on the "mature" profile flows. Future projects will start with zero-data scenarios — they expose the most fragile assumptions.

03

Involve Merchandising

Breuninger's buyers curate with an editorial eye that no algorithm replicates. Looping in the buying team earlier — as co-designers of the logic, not recipients of the output — would have produced richer, more defensible recommendations.
